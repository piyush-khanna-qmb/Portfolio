Research & Patents.

Work that ended up as a formal filing or a paper rather than only a machine on a table.

19 Aug 2026·App. No. 202611100293Filed · Patent application

A Modular IoT Ecosystem Comprising Independent Sensor-Relay Extension Modules

The architecture behind the Emperia automation ecosystem: a hub that stays deliberately simple, and sensor/relay modules that can be added independently without the hub needing to know in advance what they are. The claim is the extension model itself: modules that extend what the system can do without extending how complicated it is.

✓Controller General of Patents, Designs & Trade Marks

IEEE formatWritten up · Manuscript

Elevating Customer Engagement: The Role of Humanoid Robots as Receptionists

A study of what actually changes when a humanoid robot takes a receptionist's seat in an educational institution. The paper looks at multithreaded interaction handling, semantic analysis of visitor intent, and what the authors call empathetic technology, meaning the gap between a system that answers correctly and one that a person feels attended to by.
