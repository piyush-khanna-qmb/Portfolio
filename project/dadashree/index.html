Robotics · Parasdham, Junagadh

DadaShree

A wisdom tree robot deployed at Parasdham, a Jain monastery in Junagadh, Gujarat. I lived on site for two months through July and August 2025 while building it.

Deployment
Parasdham, Junagadh, Gujarat
Setting
Jain monastery
On site
July to August 2025
Type
Wisdom tree robot

The name

Dada means grandfather. Shree is the honorific. Put together it points at a respected elder figure, and that is deliberate: it tells a visitor what posture to arrive in before anyone explains anything. You do not approach a grandfather the way you approach a kiosk. In a monastery, where people come with questions they have been carrying for a while, that framing does more work than any interface could.

Living on site

I stayed at Parasdham for two months while the build came together. Working inside the place a machine is meant for is a different exercise from building it in a lab and shipping it. You see who actually walks up to it, what they ask, and how long they are willing to wait for an answer.

Junagadh in the monsoon

It was a wholesome couple of months, and a good deal of that was the place itself. The weather in Junagadh was kind, and the rains arriving over those two months were the cherry on the cake. I also climbed Girnar, which is famous for roughly ten thousand steep steps and earns the reputation somewhere around step four thousand.
